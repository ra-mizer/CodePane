Meet Rachel Mizer, Founder & Head Cook

Hi, I’m Rachel, and I absolutely love to cook. My happiest hours are spent in the kitchen with music playing, a pot simmering on the stove and flour somewhere it definitely shouldn’t be. What started as a way to feed myself on a budget turned into a lifelong obsession with learning how people all over the world put dinner on the table.

These days I cook just about everything: Japanese teriyaki on a Monday, an Indian chana masala on Wednesday, Mexican street tacos for friends on Friday and a big Greek salad whenever the tomatoes are good. Every cuisine has taught me something new, whether it’s a spice trick, a knife technique or a simple shortcut that makes a dish feel special.

I created The Recipe Hub for the cook I used to be: curious, a little nervous and not sure where to start. Every recipe here is tested in my own small kitchen, written in plain language and paired with a beginner tip so you can cook with confidence. My hope is that you’ll find a dish you love, make it your own and then go explore a brand-new cuisine next week.
